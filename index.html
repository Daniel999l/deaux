exhibit a: the record

the founder confirmed it himself. so did the other guy.

April 2018: Jeff Yan leaves Hudson River Trading with his college roommate, Brian Wong. They build a decentralized prediction market: bet on weather, elections, sports, anything with a result. The mechanism (off-chain matching, on-chain settlement) is the same core idea that later powers Hyperliquid's order book.

They get into Binance Labs' first-ever incubator cohort in San Francisco. They call it Deaux.

It never leaves the ground. Bitcoin is down 80% from its peak. Nobody wants to bet on tomorrow's weather. Deaux tops out around 100 users. Yan personally refunds most of the $450K raised. The project shuts down.

Four years later, same core idea, same founder, better timing: Hyperliquid launches. Today it clears more perp volume than most centralized exchanges. $DEAUX is the ticker that was never given the chance the first time.
